Bots
• On your turn, click a card in your hand, then click a bot to ask for that rank.
• If they have any, they hand them all over and you go again.
• If not, "Go fish!": you draw a card. If you fish the rank you asked for, you go again.
• 4 of a kind makes a book. Most books when all 13 are made wins.
