You made it.
Somehow, against all odds, you escaped the mainland. Tinky Winky's footsteps faded behind you as the old satellite station emerged from the fog. 
Rusted dishes creaked in the wind above, still pointing toward a sky that no longer seemed to answer.
For the first time in hours, you thought you were safe. Then you heard movement. Not outside.
Inside.
Something else had made this place its home. Something that watched from the dark corners of the station, peeking from behind broken equipment and shattered monitors.
Something that couldn't stop staring at your head. It tilts its own misshapen neck whenever it sees you, studying you. Admiring you.
Jealous of you. Because unlike it...
you still have one.
